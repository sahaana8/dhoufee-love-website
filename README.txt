HOW TO USE YOUR LOVE WEBSITE
============================

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome (or any browser). That's it!

ADD YOUR PHOTOS
- Put your photos inside the "photos" folder.
- Name them 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ... 6.jpg  (or open script.js and edit the PHOTOS list
  at the top to use your own file names and captions).

ADD YOUR SONG
- Copy Enthara-Enthara.mp3 (from your Downloads folder) into the "music" folder.
  Keep the file name exactly: Enthara-Enthara.mp3
- It plays when he taps "Open your gift". The round music button (top right) pauses it.

EDIT THE WORDS
- Love letter, our story, promises, and dreams: open index.html in Notepad and change the text.
- Reasons I love you, photo captions, and the anniversary date: top of script.js.

SHARE IT AS A SURPRISE (free options)
- Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop): drag the whole folder in, get a link, send it to him.
- Or GitHub Pages / Vercel.
- Or just open it on your phone or laptop and hand it to him.
